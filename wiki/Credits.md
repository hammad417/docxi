# docxi maintainers and credits

docxi has been built by several people since 2015. This page records who maintains it now, where it came from, and who has contributed.

## Maintainer

docxi is maintained by [Hammad Maqbool](https://github.com/hammad417) at [Phaedra Solutions](https://www.phaedrasolutions.com/). The gem on RubyGems and the GitHub repository belong to the `hammad417` account.

## History

- **2015:** Timur Gabdrakipov writes [docxer](https://github.com/gtimti/docxer), a Ruby gem for generating `.docx` files, released under the MIT license.
- **2016:** docxer is renamed to docxi, adapted and published as docxi 0.0.1, 0.0.2 and 0.0.6. The gemspec of those releases lists Irfan Babar as the author.
- **2026:** docxi 0.1.0 brings the gem up to date with current Ruby, Rails and rubyzip without changing the documents it generates.
